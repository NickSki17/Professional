# Deep Coat Industries

**Role:** Engineering Intern
**Dates:** May 2025 – August 2025; December 2025 – January 2026
**Location:** Sugar Grove, IL

## Overview

Worked across RF/EMI test development, mechanical design, instrumentation, experimental testing, fabrication, and Python-based data analysis. Developed and validated test hardware and analysis workflows for repeatable electromagnetic characterization while contributing to mechanical systems and laboratory infrastructure.

## Engineering Work

### RF/EMI Test Development

* Designed, built, and validated a full-spectrum EMI/RFI test system covering **500 Hz–6.3 GHz**, including an anechoic chamber and cassette-style sample holder for rapid, repeatable testing.
* Engineered and fabricated transmitting and receiving antennas, including loop and monopole configurations, along with custom 3D-printed and machined fixtures for precise alignment and measurement repeatability.
* Calibrated and operated vector network analyzers (VNA), oscilloscopes, RF amplifiers, and related instrumentation to collect S-parameter and time-domain measurements.
* Investigated grounding and common-mode noise in the measurement system, identifying and mitigating ground-loop effects to improve RF measurement quality and repeatability.
* Characterized electromagnetic behavior across sub-GHz and multi-GHz frequency ranges, including near-field and grounding effects.

### Mechanical Design & Fabrication

* Led mechanical design of a compact **90° rotary mechanism under tight spatial constraints**, including full AutoCAD modeling, detailed part and assembly drawings, manufacturing documentation, and assembly procedures.
* Designed precision mechanical fixtures for repeatable positioning, fitment, and alignment of experimental hardware.
* Applied design-for-manufacturing considerations to custom fixtures and laboratory hardware.
* Performed hands-on fabrication including plasma cutting, welding, soldering, machining, and 3D printing.
* Documented mechanical designs, fabrication procedures, and test hardware configurations for reproducibility and handoff.

### Data Analysis & Automation

* Developed Python tools to automate portions of instrument control, data acquisition, data organization, and experimental analysis.
* Processed experimental datasets and generated engineering visualizations including plots, trendlines, and comparative analyses.
* Developed physics-based predictive models using **recursive impedance accumulation** to estimate multilayer shielding performance across frequency ranges beyond directly measured conditions.
* Applied first-principles electromagnetic models to interpret experimental results and validate analytical calculations.
* Improved the consistency and efficiency of experimental analysis by standardizing data-processing and reporting workflows.

### Materials & Experimental Characterization

* Developed and experimentally evaluated RF absorber materials applied to foam panels, investigating frequency-dependent absorption behavior and changes in measured electromagnetic performance.
* Designed controlled test configurations to improve repeatability when comparing electromagnetic materials and configurations.
* Combined theoretical modeling, instrumentation, physical testing, and data analysis to investigate electromagnetic behavior.
