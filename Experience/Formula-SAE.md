# Formula SAE Chassis Design - Illinois Tech

**Role:** Chassis Design Team Member  
**Dates:** August 2026 – Present  
**Location:** Chicago, IL

## Overview

Developing a **carbon-fiber Formula SAE monocoque chassis** in a six-person design team, translating competition regulations, driver-template constraints, and structural requirements into preliminary CAD geometry and chassis architecture.

## Engineering Work

### Chassis Design & CAD

* Developing a **carbon-fiber monocoque chassis** in SolidWorks, translating Formula SAE requirements and structural constraints into preliminary CAD geometry.
* Developing preliminary geometry for the **front bulkhead, front hoop, side-impact structure, cockpit, impact attenuator region, and surrounding chassis structure**.
* Applying the **Percy driver template** and Formula SAE regulations to establish driver clearance, chassis geometry, and dimensional constraints.
* Using SolidWorks sketches, extrudes, cuts, lofts, surfaces, fillets, and assemblies to develop the preliminary chassis model.
* Developing a preliminary monocoque

### Structural Design & Requirements

* Translating Formula SAE chassis regulations into design constraints for the **front bulkhead, front hoop, side-impact structure, and driver compartment**.
* Developing chassis geometry with consideration for **structural requirements, driver packaging, and manufacturability**.
* Establishing the preliminary chassis architecture for subsequent structural analysis and validation.

### Composite Design

* Developing the chassis around a **carbon-fiber composite monocoque** architecture.
* Evaluating preliminary **material, manufacturing, tooling, and cost considerations** for the composite chassis.
* Reviewing Formula SAE **Structural Equivalency Spreadsheet (SES)** requirements applicable to the carbon-fiber structure.

## Technical Skills

* **CAD & Design:** SolidWorks, 3D CAD, assemblies, surface modeling, mechanical design
* **Formula SAE:** FSAE rules, chassis requirements, Percy driver template, driver packaging
* **Structural Engineering:** Chassis architecture, structural requirements, load paths, preliminary FEA
* **Composite Structures:** Carbon-fiber monocoques, composite design, SES requirements
* **Engineering:** Dimensional constraints, manufacturability, structural validation planning
